# Workshop 301 — Advanced Interviewing

For experienced interviewers ready to tackle complex scenarios. Full interview loops, cross-company frameworks, and a capstone mock interview.

## What you'll learn

| Section | Topic | Time |
|---------|-------|------|
| 1 | The Full Interview Loop | 15 min |
| 2 | Frameworks Beyond Amazon | 15 min |
| 3 | Technical Interview Integration | 15 min |
| 4 | Difficult Scenarios | 15 min |
| 5 | Building a Story Bank | 15 min |
| 6 | Capstone: Full Mock Interview | 30 min |

## Prerequisites

Complete [Workshop 201](/docs/workshop-201/) first, or have significant experience as a behavioral interviewer.
