# Interview Question Bank

A curated collection of behavioral interview questions organized by principle. Each question includes **what to look for** in the candidate's answer and **follow-up ideas** to probe deeper.

These questions are drawn from best practices across the industry — not specific to any one company. They're designed to be open enough for any candidate while targeted enough to generate real signal on a specific principle.

**How to use this page:**
- Select a principle to see 2-3 proven questions for it
- Before your interview, pick 1-2 questions for each principle you're evaluating
- Use the "What to look for" as your evaluation guide — not a checklist, but signal markers
- Use the "Follow-up ideas" when the candidate's answer has gaps or you want to go deeper
