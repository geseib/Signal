# Workshop 201 — Principles & Interviewer Skills

Take your interviewing to the next level. Learn to use **your organization's guiding principles** as evaluation lenses, write great behavioral questions, and run an effective debrief.

## What you'll learn

| Section | Topic | Time |
|---------|-------|------|
| 1 | Your Organization's Principles | 15 min |
| 2 | Mapping Stories to Principles | 20 min |
| 3 | Probing & Follow-ups | 15 min |
| 4 | Writing Great Questions | 15 min |
| 5 | Active Listening & Note-Taking | 15 min |
| 6 | Avoiding Bias | 15 min |
| 7 | The Debrief | 15 min |
| 8 | Capstone: Practice Debrief | 20 min |

## Prerequisites

Complete [Workshop 101](/docs/workshop-101) first, or have equivalent experience with STAR-based interviewing.
